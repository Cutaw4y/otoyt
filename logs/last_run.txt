Underwater earthquakes, submarine landslides, and even hungry deep sea sharks nibbling on the plastic insulation can cause failures.   [31] To protect millions of dollars of infrastructure, modern submarine cables include an extra metallic tape shield just beneath the outer jacket. When a shark bites into this heavy metal layer, it cannot pierce through to the sensitive inner layers, protecting the line.
  [32] As global demand for clean energy grows, submarine power cables are becoming the invisible highways of the future. Massive offshore wind farms hundreds of miles out at sea rely on these exact lines to bring electricity back to our homes.
